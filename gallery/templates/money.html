How these numbers work

Revenue / spend compares earnings and acquisition spend recorded during the same dates. Revenue includes organic users and users acquired earlier; it is not cohort ROAS. 1.000× means revenue equals spend.

The best-ad ratio uses attributed ad revenue only, never installs, clicks, purchase revenue, or AdMob revenue split in proportion to spend. Meta attribution uses delivery dates with a 7-day click / 1-day view window. Recent delivery may not have matured. Google App campaign spend is included in totals; without ad-level revenue attribution it cannot establish a winning ad.

Historical Dog builds used Bird’s AdMob identity. Both-game revenue still includes those earnings once, but the per-game split follows the provider app identity and may be contaminated.

Coverage: configured Meta and Google Ads accounts, and AdMob mediation earnings for these two games. In-app purchases and other business costs are excluded. Provider earnings are estimates and may be revised. Reports refresh on the next visit after 15 minutes.
